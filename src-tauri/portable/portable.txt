Aqueli - portable version

This file makes Aqueli portable. Keep it next to Aqueli.exe.

Everything stays in this folder:
  Data         your accounts, encrypted database, settings and backups
  Documents    the Import folder and the import guide

To use Aqueli on another PC or from a USB drive, quit Aqueli first
(right-click its icon near the clock, then "Quit Aqueli"), then copy the whole folder.
Your password always opens your account. Turn Windows Hello on again
on each PC, in Settings > Security.

Aqueli is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later), made by Areemi.
https://github.com/Areemi-app/aqueli

Tout reste dans ce dossier :
  Data         vos comptes, la base chiffrée, les réglages et les sauvegardes
